The Struggle Every Host Knows

Hosting workshops should be exciting, not an administrative headache.

When you run your bookings manually through WhatsApp DMs and spreadsheets, your energy is drained before you even teach your first session.

"Half the people who promised to come never show up."

People get busy, forget, or ghost. Without automated seat confirmations and 24h reminders, 30% to 50% of your confirmed attendees turn into empty seats.

"I spend hours checking bank alerts and chasing balances."

Searching banking apps for matching transaction references, verifying screenshots, and sending awkward follow-up DMs for installment payments is exhausting.

"My WhatsApp is completely flooded with repetitive queries."

"Is there still space?", "What is your account number?", "Where is the venue map?" You answer the same questions 40 times while important messages get lost.

Built For Turnout

Everything you need to keep students showing up.

Designed specifically around the daily habits of African students and workshop hosts.

WhatsApp & SMS reminders that students actually act on

Emails get buried in spam. TuitEase sends personal reminders directly via WhatsApp (with automatic SMS fallback) so students never miss a session.

Confirm-your-seat nudges & automatic waitlist

3 days before class, students receive a prompt: "Reply YES to confirm your seat." If an unpaid student drops out, the seat automatically passes to the waitlist.

Installments with automatic polite follow-ups

Higher-ticket classes fill up faster when students can split payments. TuitEase manages due dates and sends friendly payment nudges with instant 1-click Paystack links.

Bank transfer proof matching & manual logs

Have students who prefer manual bank transfer? Mark them as paid in one click. TuitEase updates their balance, clears their installment, and issues an instant receipt.

Multi-week sessions & attendance tracking

Running a 4-week cohort? Track attendance per session in seconds. Automatically send "Sorry you missed Session 2" catch-up messages with replay links to absentees.

Online and offline venue support

Works equally well for Zoom webinars or in-person workshops. Include Google Maps links, parking instructions, and studio check-in codes in confirmations.
